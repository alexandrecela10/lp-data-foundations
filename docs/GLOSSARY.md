# Glossary (plain words)

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| LP | An investor that commits money to private equity funds run by managers | Aldermoor Pension Plan (invented) |
| Fund of funds | A fund that invests in other funds | Selbrook Fund of Funds III holds 5% of Ostrova Capital Partners IV |
| Co-investment | A direct stake in one company, made alongside a fund | Our \$5m stake in Calderwick Holdings next to Halvane V |
| Stake bought second-hand | A share of a fund bought from another investor, not committed at launch | 3% of Tellmar Capital Partners III, bought 30 June 2024 |
| Look-through exposure | Our share of a company's value, through every route we hold it | \$8.06m in Calder Packaging through 5 routes |
| Route | A chain of holdings from us to a company | Aldermoor > Selbrook FoF III > Ostrova IV > Calder Pack. > Calder Packaging Ltd |
| Holding company | A company whose only asset is another company | Calderwick Holdings owns Calder Packaging Ltd |
| Identity matching | Deciding that two spellings name the same company | "Calder Pack." is Calder Packaging Ltd |
| Write-down | A manager cuts a company's value | Calder Packaging marked at 0.40 times its 30 June value |
| Point in time | Answering with only what we knew on a date | As of 9 September the write-down didn't count; as of 10 September it did |
| Net TVPI | (Cash back + current value) divided by cash paid in, after fees | 1.50x on the 2019 to 2021 buyout funds |
| DPI | Cash back divided by cash paid in | |
| Net IRR | The yearly return that makes our dated cash flows net to zero | Ours, not the manager's figure |
| Unfunded | What we promised but haven't been asked for yet | v2 adds back distributions the fund may call again |
| Semantic layer | A list of governed metrics, each defined once with an owner, that every tool asks | Chat, dashboard and notebook show the same \$8.06m |
| Graph | Things plus the links between them; questions become walks along links | People who left Brennick and joined another of our managers |
| Bronze, silver, gold | Data as received; checked and read into fields; clean tables everyone uses | |
| Lineage | The document behind each number | `REP:F0002:2026-06-30` |
| Mechanism test | An offline run where fixed readers stand in for the AI | What each setup can and can't do, not Claude's error rate |
