The Ardenvale Family Foundation was registered on 12 March 2024. The founder is Karim Ardenvale.

The beneficiaries are Leila Ardenvale and Omar Ardenvale, in equal shares. The foundation holds the family's private markets portfolio and the Dubai residence.

The foundation council has three members: Karim Ardenvale, Priya Kestrell and one independent council member.
